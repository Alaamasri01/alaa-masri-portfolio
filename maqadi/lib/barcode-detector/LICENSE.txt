Files in this folder are copied unchanged from npm packages, for scanning
barcodes on browsers without a built-in BarcodeDetector (Safari on iPhone).

  ponyfill.js, zxing-exported.js  barcode-detector 3.2.5   MIT
  zxing_reader.wasm               zxing-wasm 3.1.8         MIT (built from zxing-cpp, Apache-2.0)
