Concept A — Architectural Editorial00 · Design system

Concept

A quiet architecture monograph. Warm stone paper, black typography and a Swiss grid give the work the neutrality of a museum catalogue, so the renders are read as architecture rather than advertising. Images sit inside the margins like plates in a book, always at native proportion. Large serif titles give the voice; small sans-serif data tables carry the facts. Bronze appears only as a hairline or a single word, a few times in the whole book.
