## CONCEPT A — Architectural Editorial

A quiet architecture monograph. Warm stone paper, black typography and a Swiss 12-column grid. Renders sit inside the margins like plates in a book, never bled or cropped. Large serif titles give the voice, and small sans-serif data tables carry the facts. Bronze appears only as a hairline.

- **Cover:** typography only, with no image.
- **Grid:** 12 columns × 10.67 mm, 4 mm gutter. Margins: top 18, bottom 22, inner 22, outer 16 mm.
- **Images:** 172 mm plates (≈ 265 ppi) or 84 mm two-ups (≈ 400 ppi). No crops.
- **Paper:** ivory uncoated stock.

## CONCEPT B — Dark Cinematic

The website translated into print. The pages are olive-black. The renders run edge to edge as 16 : 9 cinematic bands, so the light in each image is the main event. Typography is oversized and ivory, with bronze kept for numerals and captions.

- **Cover:** one strong render (skyline-suite-01) as a full-bleed band.
- **Grid:** 6 columns × 25.2 mm, 5 mm gutter. Margins: top 16, bottom 18, inner 20, outer 14 mm.
- **Images:** full-bleed bands, 210 mm wide (≈ 218 ppi). No crops.
- **Print:** heavy ink coverage, so it needs coated silk stock and a matt-laminated cover. This is the direction that benefits most from high-resolution originals.

## CONCEPT C — Contemporary Luxury

An interiors-magazine reading of the work. It uses light paper, very large serif type split between roman and italic, and asymmetric compositions. Each image is played against a single warm sand block. Images bleed off one edge only, and page furniture is reduced to the page number.

- **Cover:** name split around one image (burgundy-salon-01), which bleeds off the right edge (≈ 295 ppi).
- **Grid:** 8 columns × 18 mm, 4 mm gutter. Margins: top 14, bottom 16, inner 24, outer 14 mm.
- **Images:** 145–190 mm (≈ 240–310 ppi). There is one controlled square crop (walnut-suite-05, ≈ 284 ppi).

## Rules applied in all three concepts

- **Fonts:** Instrument Serif + Inter, the website fonts. Both are open-licence (SIL OFL), so they can be embedded in the PDFs. No alternative is recommended: Instrument Serif prints well at display sizes, and it is never used below 13 pt.
- **Titles:** "ALAA MASRI — Interior Designer / Interior Architect". No studio name and no logo.
- **Year:** "Portfolio 2026–2027".
- **Profile:** first person, word for word from the website.
- **Facts:** every fact comes from the profile, CV or website.
- **Missing project data:** shown as a discreet muted "To confirm" under its label (Location, Year, Status, Role, Scope). Design intent is reserved as one muted line.
- **Technical capability:** six empty, labelled sheets — Plans, Elevations/Sections, Shop drawings, Joinery details, BOQ / material schedule, Execution & site documentation. **No drawings are simulated.**
- **Experience:** not shown in Phase 3. It will be a restrained overview with placeholders until employers and dates are provided.
- **Image scaling:** no image is upscaled beyond ~218 ppi. No full-height portrait crops are taken from landscape renders. Nothing crosses the spine. All images keep their native proportions except the one stated crop.
