## How the hybrid is controlled

- **Concept C (70%) — the identity.** Covers, profile, project spreads and contact. It supplies the ivory paper, oversized serif titles split between roman and italic, one sand block per page, and images bleeding off a single outer edge.
- **Concept A (20%) — wherever the book states facts.** Metadata rows, "Fig." captions, folios, the expertise table and the technical drawing register. It uses the same fonts and colours as the rest of the book, so these pages stay precise without looking corporate.
- **Concept B (10%) — pauses.** Used for at most one dark opener in every two projects, for section dividers, for no more than two dark image spreads, and for the back cover. Dark pages keep the same grid and type; only the palette inverts. There are never two dark spreads in a row.
