## What changed from Phase 4

- **Density:** project spreads now carry about 65–85% content. Empty ivory remains only where it frames something, such as the title zones and the field above the portrait.
- **Project case study (The walnut suite):** opener → design language → space study → elevations → detail and materiality. The same structure will apply to the other strong projects, scaled to how much imagery each has.
- **Detail extraction:** enlarged crops of joinery, lit reveals, the marble ledge, the headboard wall and wardrobe bays, all cut from your renders. Every crop prints at ≥ 230 ppi except the 15 mm material-board swatches (203–271 ppi, fine at that size).
- **Material boards:**
  - Texture swatches are real pixel crops of your renders.
  - Colours are medians sampled from named regions of named renders (`sample_palette.py` records each file and region).
  - The skyline suite strip uses your own material board, labelled exactly as on the board. The board's own spelling "PANELING" is shown as "panelling" to match the book.
- **CAD-style graphics:** precise vector linework drawn with line weights, hatches (masonry, concrete/gypsum, timber, upholstery, stone), dimension lines, grid bubbles, elevation markers, numbered bronze callouts and title blocks. Every drawing relates to the images on its page.
