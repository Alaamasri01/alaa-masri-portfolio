02Profile

Imagination. With the knowledge to make it real.

I’m Alaa Masri, a Lebanese interior designer and interior architect based in Riyadh. Over 15 years of practice across the Gulf and Lebanon have shaped my approach to space.

I work from the first concept through to final handover, connecting design, materials and craftsmanship with the precision that execution demands.

My work spans luxury residential, hospitality, commercial and retail interiors — coordinating clients, consultants, contractors, workshops, factories and site teams so that the design holds from drawing to site.

03Expertise

Every space. Every detail.

From the first sketch to the final handover — design authorship backed by the technical and site knowledge to deliver it.

  1. 01

    Interior design

    Creative, functional concepts for luxury residential, commercial and retail interiors — planned around the way each space will be lived in and used.

    • Space planning & furniture layouts
    • Concept design & mood boards
    • Material & finish selection
    • Furniture design & custom joinery
  2. 02

    Interior architecture

    The architectural elements of the interior — entrances, ceilings, partitions and feature walls — designed as one coherent whole.

    • Entrance & foyer design
    • Decorative ceilings
    • Feature walls & partitions
    • Staircase areas
  3. 03

    Technical & shop drawings

    Preparing technical drawings and coordinating shop drawings so that the design can be built exactly as intended.

    • Technical drawings
    • Shop drawing coordination
    • Construction & execution details
  4. 04

    BOQ & cost estimation

    Bills of quantities, take-offs and cost estimates, with interior works priced and quotations coordinated with suppliers and subcontractors.

    • BOQ & quantity take-off
    • Cost estimation & pricing
    • Supplier quotations
  5. 05

    Fit-out & coordination

    Coordinating clients, contractors, suppliers, factories and site teams, and reviewing material samples against the project specifications.

    • Stone, marble & glass
    • Steel & metal fabrication
    • Lighting coordination
  6. 06

    Site supervision

    Following execution on site, resolving technical and design issues and maintaining quality from design approval through to final handover.

    • Handover

Process — from vision to reality

One vision. Every step.

  1. 01

    Envision

    Listen. Explore. Find the direction.

    Understand the brief, study the space and develop a concept that balances aesthetics with everyday function.

  2. 02

    Develop

    Refine the idea. Resolve the details.

    Translate the vision into layouts, materials, technical drawings and clear quantities and cost estimates.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    Coordinate. Bring it to life.

    Work with clients, contractors, suppliers and site teams to resolve challenges and maintain quality through handover.
