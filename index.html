03Profile

Imagination. With the knowledge to make it real.

I’m Alaa Masri, a Lebanese interior designer and interior architect based in Riyadh. Over 15 years of practice across the Gulf and Lebanon have shaped my approach to space.

I work from the first concept through to final handover, connecting design, materials and craftsmanship with the precision that execution demands.

My work spans luxury residential, hospitality, commercial and retail interiors — coordinating clients, consultants, contractors, workshops, factories and site teams so that the design holds from drawing to site.

Process — from vision to reality

One vision. Every step.

  1. 01Envision

    Listen. Explore. Find the direction.

    Understand the brief, study the space and develop a concept that balances aesthetics with everyday function.

  2. 02Develop

    Refine the idea. Resolve the details.

    Translate the vision into layouts, materials, technical drawings and clear quantities and cost estimates.

  3. 03Deliver

    Coordinate. Bring it to life.

    Work with clients, contractors, suppliers and site teams to resolve challenges and maintain quality through handover.
